How it works

Four steps

  1. We asked the AIs. ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini, the questions people ask about a city: hidden gems, date nights, where locals go. Every answer is stored with its model and date.
  2. We crossed out every place they named. Famous or tiny, if an AI named it, it’s out.
  3. Qloo ranks the rest for your loves. Three things you love, an artist, a film, a dish, become Qloo taste signals; Qloo scores every place left for each of them.
  4. We keep what fits you, not everyone. The seven are the places that fit you much more than they fit most people’s taste, or than their popularity alone would say. Rated 4.3+, open, nearby. No place can pay to be picked.

How it works

Four steps

  1. We asked the AIs. ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini, the questions people ask about a city: hidden gems, date nights, where locals go. Every answer is stored with its model and date.
  2. We crossed out every place they named. Famous or tiny, if an AI named it, it’s out.
  3. Qloo ranks the rest for your loves. Qloo scores every place left for each of your three loves: an artist, a film, a dish.
  4. We keep what fits you, not everyone. The seven fit you far better than they fit everyone else. Rated 4.3+, nearby. No place can pay to be picked.
